Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, one playthrough, no difficulty trophies and no online trophies. The story takes 60–80 hours; the 100% checklist is the long grind after it. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of each chapter.

⚠ One trophy may be missable. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia may offer to meditate on the balcony at Jason's house. Accept it — Positive Mental Attitude may need that session, and it is gone after Chapter 1. Every other trophy stays earnable in free roam after the story.
